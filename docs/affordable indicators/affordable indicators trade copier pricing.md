FAQ
Trade Copier Questions, Answered
What is a trade copier?
A trade copier automatically replicates the trades you place in one leader account across one or more follower accounts in real time. You trade once and it mirrors your entries, exits, stops and targets everywhere — so prop firm traders can manage multiple funded accounts at the same time without placing every order by hand.
Is a trade copier allowed by prop firms?
Most futures prop firms — including Apex, Topstep, Tradeify, Take Profit, Bulenox and Lucid — allow you to copy your own trades across your own accounts, which is exactly what our copier does. Rules vary by firm and can change, so always confirm your firm's current policy. Our copier runs locally with per-account risk controls, keeping you inside typical prop-firm requirements.
Which platforms and data feeds does it support?
NinjaTrader, TradingView, Tradovate and Rithmic. You can copy between accounts on the same platform or across platforms.
How fast is the copying?
Copying happens in real time — the instant your leader account fills or places an order, the copier mirrors it to your follower accounts automatically.
What is the difference between Orders Mode and Executions Mode?
Orders Mode mirrors your full order structure (entries, stops, targets, modifications and cancellations) to follower accounts — best for normal trade management. Executions Mode fires an immediate market order on the follower the instant your leader fills — best for scalping and fast strategies where raw speed matters most.
Is this a monthly subscription?
No. You pay once and own it for life — no monthly fees and no recurring billing. The Trade Copier is a one-time $175, or $295 for the full Accounts Dashboard Suite (copier plus risk manager). Free updates and support are included.
Can I copy across multiple prop firm accounts at once?
Yes. You can link and copy to multiple funded and personal accounts simultaneously, each with its own position sizing and risk settings, so a single trade scales across your whole account portfolio.
Do I need a VPS?
No — it runs on your own PC. Many prop traders run it on a low-cost VPS so copying continues 24/7 even when their home machine is off.
