Refund Policy

Last updated: 2024-01-01  ·  Prop Trader Tools
14-day money-back guarantee on lifetime licenses. If PTT Trade Copier doesn't work for your setup, email us within 14 days for a full refund — no questions asked.

We want you to be confident in your purchase. This policy explains exactly when refunds are available, how to request one, and what to expect.

Lifetime License Refunds

Lifetime licenses are eligible for a full refund if requested within 14 calendar days of the purchase date.

To request a refund:

  • Email support@proptradertools.com with the subject line "Refund Request"
  • Include your order number (from your LemonSqueezy receipt email)
  • Briefly describe why the product didn't work for you (optional, but helps us improve)

We process refund requests within 2 business days. The refund is issued to your original payment method via LemonSqueezy. Your license key will be deactivated upon refund.

Monthly Subscription Refunds

Monthly subscriptions are not eligible for partial or pro-rated refunds for the current billing period. When you cancel:

  • Your subscription stops renewing immediately
  • Your access continues until the end of the current billing period
  • No further charges are made
Note: If you were charged in error (e.g., you cancelled and were still billed), contact us within 7 days and we will issue a full refund for that charge.

Exceptions — When Refunds May Be Denied

We reserve the right to deny a refund if:

  • The 14-day window has passed (lifetime licenses)
  • There is evidence that the license key was shared, distributed, or used by more than one person
  • A chargeback was filed with your bank or payment provider — please contact us first; chargebacks result in permanent account and license revocation
  • The purchase was made through an unauthorized reseller

How Refunds Are Processed

All payments are processed by LemonSqueezy (our Merchant of Record). Refunds are issued via LemonSqueezy back to your original payment method. Processing time depends on your bank or card network — typically 5–10 business days after we initiate the refund.

No Questions Asked

Within 14 days, we will refund you without requiring a reason. You do not need to prove the Software doesn't work. You do not need to troubleshoot with support first. Simply email us and the refund will be issued.

We prefer happy customers over held revenue.
