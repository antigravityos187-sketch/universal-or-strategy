Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I pay?
LemonSqueezy processes the payment and fires a webhook that generates your Cryptolens license key. You receive an email with your key and a link to download the zip. Delivery is automated — typically under 60 seconds.
How do I install the license key?
After importing the zip into NT8, open Tools → Add-Ons → NinjaTrader Trade Copier and paste your key into the license field. It validates remotely once, then caches locally for 7 days.
Does the monthly subscription auto-renew?
Yes. LemonSqueezy charges your card monthly and the webhook extends your license by 30 days on each successful payment. If you cancel, access continues until the end of your current billing period.
Can I upgrade from monthly to lifetime later?
Yes. Purchase the lifetime license at any time — your previous monthly charges are not credited, but you stop being billed. Contact support if you need help switching keys.
Is the WSGTA code really 50% off forever?
Yes. Code wsgta gives $100 off the $200 lifetime price — no expiry, no tricks. WSGTA members who buy with this code are grandfathered: they get all future v1.x updates and all features forever, regardless of any future tier changes we make for new buyers.
What is the refund policy?
14-day money-back guarantee for lifetime licenses. Monthly subscriptions are not refunded for the current billing period. See our full Refund Policy.
Do I need to do anything for VAT?
No. LemonSqueezy is the Merchant of Record — they collect and remit EU VAT, UK VAT, Australian GST, and all other applicable taxes on your behalf. You receive a clean payment.
